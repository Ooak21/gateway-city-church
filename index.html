Campus pastors

Raise up champions.

Lead pastors

Pastor Danny and Margaret Hand

Pastor Danny and Margaret Hand have been pastoring in Las Vegas for 23 years. They believe Christians are to be the best of the best, free from addictions and all forms of bondage. Danny is a coach at heart who wants to pull God's potential out of everybody who wants to be discipled.

Our mission is simply to Raise Up Champions for Christ.

Pastoral team

Pastor Paul

Pastor Paul serves on the GateWay Las Vegas pastoral team, helping care for people and advance the mission of the church. He is committed to raising up believers who love God, love people, and walk in freedom.

Pastoral team

Pastor Fred Corona

Pastor Fred Corona serves alongside the Hand family, helping lead and care for the GateWay Las Vegas family. He has a heart for people, for the local church, and for seeing lives transformed by Jesus right here in our city.

GateWay Vegas is one of seven GateWay City Church campuses across California, Arizona, and Nevada, part of a family led by Pastors David and Kathy Cannistraci since 1966.
