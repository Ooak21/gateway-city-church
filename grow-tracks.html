Grow Tracks.

Four sessions. Connection, freedom, purpose, and the Holy Spirit. No homework. No pressure to keep pace. Kids are welcome during services.

01 · Connection

Getting Connected

How to build a relationship with God and how to get connected here at GateWay. This is where your journey begins, meeting the people who will walk alongside you.

02 · Freedom

Freedom and Wholeness

Gaining freedom from your past is one of your first steps into your future with God. Discover what true freedom and wholeness look like, and how Jesus Christ can set you free.

03 · Purpose

Discovering Your SHAPE

Who God created you to be, and His unique plan for your life: Spiritual gifts, Heart, Abilities, Personality, and Experiences.

04 · The Holy Spirit

Baptism of the Holy Spirit

We are connected to God continually because of the presence of the Holy Spirit. Who He is, and how to receive the Baptism of the Holy Spirit.
